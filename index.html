Our Heritage

The Philosophy of Dum Pukht

At ZAFRAN, we believe that true culinary luxury cannot be rushed. Our kitchen honors the ancient Persian technique of Dum Pukht—literally translated as "breathing and slow cooking"—where the food cooks in its own natural juices without artificial additives.

Every ingredient is hand-selected: from authentic Mogra saffron threads hand-picked in Pampore to whole spices sourced directly from smallholder artisan farmers across the Indian subcontinent.
